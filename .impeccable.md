## Design Context

### Users
Mixed Indian market — middle-aged homemakers/spiritual seekers (35–55, phone-first, Instagram/WhatsApp-driven) and working professionals (25–45, urban, sceptical but curious). They are looking for a credible expert guide to help with life decisions. Context: landing on a high-ticket (₹5,999) payment form after seeing content about numerology and destiny.

### Brand Personality
**Wise, grounded, transformative.** The Batraa Numerology positions as a respected authority — not a hustle brand, not a spiritual vibe brand. Think: credentialed expert's consultation room, engraved certificate, a therapist's letterhead.

### Aesthetic Direction
Premium coaching/consultancy — authoritative and calm. Reference: Tony Robbins meets a well-printed Indian expert's certificate. Light mode. Warm palette: deep burgundy (`#8B0F2E`) as brand, amber (`#F5A03A`) reserved for the single payment CTA (Von Restorff). Warm off-white background (`#F7F4F1`), slightly tinted card surfaces.

**Fonts:** Gloock (display/headings) — high-contrast editorial serif, 2023 Google Fonts, feels like credentialed printed matter. Figtree (body) — warm rounded grotesque, mobile-friendly, not overused.

**Anti-reference:** Generic "spiritual" pastels, cyan-on-dark AI aesthetic, glassmorphism.

### Design Principles
1. **Authority through restraint** — the form earns trust by being calm and precise, not by shouting urgency (urgency mechanics exist but are subtle).
2. **Warm but credentialed** — every typographic and colour choice should feel like a respected professional's materials, not a sales page.
3. **Mobile-first** — majority of traffic is phone. Touch targets, readable labels, clean 1-column stacking on small screens.
4. **One accent colour** — amber is reserved exclusively for the payment CTA. Everything else uses brand burgundy or neutrals.
5. **No side-stripe accents** — box differentiation through elevation (shadow) and background tint, never `border-left` colour bars.
